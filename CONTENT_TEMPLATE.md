# Website Content Template

Thank you for helping us build your website. This document lists everything we need from you, page by page and section by section. Fill in the **Your content** column. Where a section repeats (team members, services, testimonials), copy the rows as many times as you need.

## Before you start

- **We use your words exactly as written.** We won't rewrite, shorten or add to your text, so please send it final and proofread.
- **Length guides are recommendations.** The layout is designed around them. Much longer text can push the design out of shape, so if you go well over, let's talk.
- **Only fill in the pages on your site.** We agree on the list of pages (your sitemap) before you start. Skip any section for a page that isn't on it. Pages this template doesn't cover, such as a directory or product pages, go in [section 14](#14-other-pages-and-page-types).
- **Images:**
  - Send the largest original you have, at least the size listed. Landscape, portrait or square matters more than exact pixels; we'll crop to fit.
  - JPG for photos, PNG or SVG for logos and icons.
  - Only send images you have the right to use.
  - **Describe each image in one short sentence** (for example "Our team at the 2024 planning day"). This "alt text" is read aloud to people who can't see the image, and it's required for accessibility.
- **Videos:** MP4, under 10 MB each. Videos play silently in the page, so they shouldn't rely on sound.
- **Placeholders:** if you don't have something yet, write **TBD**. We'll follow up before launch rather than guess.
