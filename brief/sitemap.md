## Homepage content blocks

section order where it works; the order below is a preference, not a requirement.

1. Header: the theme's header with logo, nav, and the "Find a Trusted Business" button.
2. Hero: the theme's hero with the hero headline, hero body, and buttons "Explore Our Brands" and "Find a Trusted Business". Navy background, serif headline.
3. Lead statement: the lead message, set large. Use the theme's quote, statement or simple text section.
4. Family of brands: the theme's card or feature grid, three cards with the one-liners. Division accent colour on each card.
5. Four pillars: the theme's feature/icon grid with Trust, Recognition, Intelligence, Growth.
6. Stats: only if the theme has a stats section. All numbers are placeholders.
7. 40 years: the theme's split text-and-image section with the anniversary headline and first paragraph, image placeholder, "Read Our Story" button.
8. Who we serve: the theme's card grid with Consumers, Award Winners, and Media and Partners.
9. Testimonials: remove or hide the theme's testimonials until real quotes are supplied. Note it in gaps.md.
10. Closing CTA: the theme's CTA band with the site-wide tagline and "Contact Us" button.
11. Footer: the theme's footer with the family of brands, nav, contact placeholders, social placeholders, legal links, and the site-wide tagline.

Remove any other theme homepage sections that have no matching content.
