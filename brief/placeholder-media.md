# Placeholder media

Free-licence photos and video used as placeholders until the client supplies its own
photography. **Every file below must be replaced with the client's photography (or approved
media) before launch.** Files live in `src/assets/images/placeholders/` and
`src/assets/videos/placeholders/`.

Licences:
- **Unsplash License** (https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial and non-commercial use, no permission or attribution required. It does not allow selling unaltered copies or building a competing image service. Unsplash+ images (paid) were excluded.
- **Pexels License** (https://www.pexels.com/license/): free to use and modify, attribution not required. It does not allow selling unaltered copies, implying endorsement by people or brands shown, or redistributing on other stock sites.

Selection rules: chosen for the brief's calm, established-institution feel, Canadian settings where possible, and no visible company logos or brand names. Rejected for visible branding:
- the Yonge-Dundas Square time-lapse
- the Toronto night skyline (lit tower logos)
- the Gastown street (shop names, car badge)
- a bakery (menu screen branding)
- a café counter (logo poster)
- a historic main street (shop names)
- baristas (bottle labels)
- champagne with friends (bottle labels)

Also rejected: a Canadian flags photo taken at the 2022 convoy protest, as political.

Hero imagery (bold-round), also rejected:
- the first version A photo, Old Québec and the Château Frontenac at dusk: the Château is a hotel
  brand's building (replaced with Seaton Lake, on the project owner's request)
- Vermilion Lakes (Banff) sunsets: bright, saturated skies that need a much heavier overlay
- an Old Québec dusk skyline with "Delta" and "Hilton" signs on two towers
- a Montréal dusk aerial dominated by the Farine Five Roses sign
- a Vancouver harbour sunset with a scrolling LED sign on BC Place
- Seaton Lake (Lillooet, BC) at dusk, version A until 2026-10-30: replaced, on the project owner's
  request, with a business event photo (people not the focal point, no logos or signage)

Business event photos for hero version A (2026-10-30), rejected:
- people mingling at a San Francisco tech event (a "Unity" logo on a bag, faces in focus)
- a crowded Gothenburg event hall (a banner, an exit sign, a market stall)
- an office social (labelled bottles on the shelves, bright daylight that needs a heavy overlay)
- three cocktail-reception and networking photos with faces in sharp focus
- a fully blurred crowd under string lights (green club-style lighting; doesn't read as business)
- every large photo on ccaward.com: licensed stock (many with the award decal added to shop
  windows), winners' ads, or graphics; no Consumer Choice Award event photography was found
- vertical (9:16) clips of Old Québec and Vancouver, which don't fit a wide hero
- Ottawa skylines (Parliament: government connection) and Toronto clips (the showreel below is already Toronto)
- a calm night waterfront in blue hour, location not stated and apparently European, so the Canadian Québec aerial was used instead
