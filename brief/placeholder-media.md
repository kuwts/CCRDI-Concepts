# Placeholder media (CCRDI)

Free-licence photos and video used as placeholders until the client supplies its own
photography (approved by the project owner, 2026-10-30). **Every file below must be replaced
with the client's photography (or approved media) before launch.** Files live in
`src/assets/images/placeholders/` and `src/assets/videos/placeholders/`.

  building a competing image service. Unsplash+ images (paid) are excluded.
- **Pexels License** (https://www.pexels.com/license/): free to use and modify, attribution not
  required. It does not allow selling unaltered copies, implying endorsement by people or brands
  shown, or redistributing on other stock sites.

Selection rules (design.md): research and data photography (people at work with data, reports,
screens and workshops), cool light that sits well with Ink and Mist, no visible company logos
or brand names, no stock handshakes, trophies or people pointing at laptops.
