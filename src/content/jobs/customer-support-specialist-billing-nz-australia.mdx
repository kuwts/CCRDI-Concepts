## About Obicol

Obicol is a creative studio that designs and builds websites, apps and brands for growing companies. We're a small, senior team that works closely with every client, from the first idea to launch and beyond.

We work remotely across several time zones and meet in person a few times a year. Our clients range from early-stage startups to established brands in retail, finance and the arts.

## About the Role

We're looking for a customer support specialist to join our billing team. You'll help our customers with invoicing, plan changes, and payment-related questions, ensuring a smooth experience for every user.

## Requirements

- 2+ years of experience in customer support, preferably in SaaS
- Experience with billing systems and payment platforms
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to troubleshoot and resolve issues independently
- Familiarity with CRM tools and ticketing systems
