## About Obicol

Obicol is a creative studio that designs and builds websites, apps and brands for growing companies. We're a small, senior team that works closely with every client, from the first idea to launch and beyond.

We work remotely across several time zones and meet in person a few times a year. Our clients range from early-stage startups to established brands in retail, finance and the arts.

## About the Role

We're looking for a customer support manager to lead our UK/Ireland team. You'll be responsible for mentoring agents, improving support workflows, and ensuring our customers receive world-class assistance.

## Requirements

- 4+ years of experience in customer support, with 2+ in a leadership role
- Experience managing remote teams
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Proficiency with support platforms and reporting tools
- Excellent communication and coaching abilities
