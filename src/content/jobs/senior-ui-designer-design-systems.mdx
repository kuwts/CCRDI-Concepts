### About Obicol

Obicol is changing the future of how videos are made by helping over 1 million creative professionals seamlessly collaborate from all over the world.

We're backed by Accel, FirstMark, Insight Partners, SignalFire, Jared Leto, and a host of other amazing investors. Our market-leading product is used and loved by companies such as Turner, Disney, NASA, Snapchat, BBC, BuzzFeed, TED, Adobe, Udemy, and many more.

### About the Role

We're looking for a senior UI designer to contribute to our design system. You'll craft pixel-perfect components, define visual patterns, and ensure a cohesive look and feel across the entire product.

### Requirements

- 4+ years of experience in UI design, with a strong portfolio
- Expertise in visual design principles, typography, and color theory
- Experience building and maintaining component libraries
- Proficiency with Figma, design tokens, and handoff workflows
- Strong attention to detail and systematic thinking
