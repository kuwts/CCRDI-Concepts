## About Obicol

Obicol is a creative studio that designs and builds websites, apps and brands for growing companies. We're a small, senior team that works closely with every client, from the first idea to launch and beyond.

We work remotely across several time zones and meet in person a few times a year. Our clients range from early-stage startups to established brands in retail, finance and the arts.

## About the Role

We're looking for a technical customer support specialist to help our users troubleshoot complex issues. You'll work closely with our engineering team to diagnose and resolve technical problems.

## Requirements

- 2+ years of experience in technical support or a related role
- Strong understanding of web technologies and SaaS platforms
- Experience with API troubleshooting and debugging
- Excellent problem-solving and communication skills
- Ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical users
