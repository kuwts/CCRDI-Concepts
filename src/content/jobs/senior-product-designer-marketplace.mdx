### About Obicol

Obicol is changing the future of how videos are made by helping over 1 million creative professionals seamlessly collaborate from all over the world.

We're backed by Accel, FirstMark, Insight Partners, SignalFire, Jared Leto, and a host of other amazing investors. Our market-leading product is used and loved by companies such as Turner, Disney, NASA, Snapchat, BBC, BuzzFeed, TED, Adobe, Udemy, and many more.

### About the Role

We're looking for a senior product designer to own the marketplace experience. You'll design intuitive experiences that connect creators with collaborators, driving engagement and transactions across our platform.

### Requirements

- 4+ years of experience in product design, preferably in marketplace or e-commerce
- Strong portfolio showcasing end-to-end design thinking
- Experience designing for web and mobile platforms
- Proficiency with Figma, prototyping tools, and design systems
- Understanding of data-driven design and A/B testing
