### About Obicol

Obicol is changing the future of how videos are made by helping over 1 million creative professionals seamlessly collaborate from all over the world.

We're backed by Accel, FirstMark, Insight Partners, SignalFire, Jared Leto, and a host of other amazing investors. Our market-leading product is used and loved by companies such as Turner, Disney, NASA, Snapchat, BBC, BuzzFeed, TED, Adobe, Udemy, and many more.

### About the Role

We're looking for a product marketing lead to own our go-to-market strategy. You'll define positioning, craft messaging, and drive launches that resonate with creative professionals worldwide.

### Requirements

- 5+ years of experience in product marketing, preferably in SaaS
- Proven track record of successful product launches
- Strong storytelling and copywriting skills
- Experience with market research and competitive analysis
- Ability to collaborate cross-functionally with product, sales, and design teams
