### About Obicol

Obicol is changing the future of how videos are made by helping over 1 million creative professionals seamlessly collaborate from all over the world.

We're backed by Accel, FirstMark, Insight Partners, SignalFire, Jared Leto, and a host of other amazing investors. Our market-leading product is used and loved by companies such as Turner, Disney, NASA, Snapchat, BBC, BuzzFeed, TED, Adobe, Udemy, and many more.

### About the Role

We're looking for a senior product design manager to lead our design systems team. You'll define and evolve our component library, ensuring consistency and scalability across all product surfaces.

### Requirements

- 6+ years of experience in product design, with 3+ in a leadership role
- Deep experience building and maintaining design systems
- Strong understanding of accessibility standards and responsive design
- Experience mentoring and managing design teams
- Proficiency with Figma, token-based theming, and design tooling
