      - question: "What do I need to prepare in order to start the 5-day design sprint with you?"
        answer: |
          <p>First, we ask you to fill out our smart questionnaire form. The main thing we get from your answers is what is your own perception of your future brand. This will give us a direction of thinking and research.</p>
          <p>The form has some additional questions that are not directly related to the visual identity but their answers will give us the whole picture about the plans for your brand—what problem do you solve and who is your customer (target audience).</p>
          <p>Second, we will need a list of identities you like and ones you don't like—and why. A list of your direct competitors will make it easier for both you and us to get on the same page faster. If you have a pitch deck already created, don't hesitate to share it with us.</p>
