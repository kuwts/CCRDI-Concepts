## Recognizing businesses that earn consumer trust

Consumer Choice Award is the recognition division of Consumer Choice Canada.

Your recognition is not based on an application, a company nominating itself, or the opinion of a small judging panel. It begins with independent consumer research and the collective voice of the marketplace.
