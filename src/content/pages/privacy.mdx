## Information we collect

We only collect the information we need to answer your questions, run our projects and improve this website.

### Information you give us

- Contact details you send through our forms, such as your name, email address, phone number and company.
- Job applications, including your CV and cover letter.
- Anything else you choose to share with us by email or phone.

### Information we collect automatically

- Basic analytics, such as the pages you visit, how long you stay and the type of device you use.
- Technical data, such as your browser and approximate location, taken from your IP address.

## How we use your information

We use your information to reply to you, to prepare proposals and deliver projects, to assess job applications and to understand how people use this website. We never sell your personal information.

1. To answer your enquiries and send you the information you ask for.
2. To manage our relationship with you as a client or applicant.
3. To keep this website secure and working well.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to two years and job applications for up to one year, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
